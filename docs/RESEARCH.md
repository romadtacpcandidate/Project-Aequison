# AEQ-RES-001 — Research Program Overview
**Rev 0.1 | Draft | 2026-10-08**

AEQUISON is an independent research program exploring the physics, numerical modeling, materials, flight systems, and feasibility questions relevant to transonic aircraft. Competition activity is tracked separately.

## Workstreams
- Atmospheric properties: reference models, units, and validation.
- Computational aerodynamics: reproduce published transonic experimental benchmarks; verify numerical convergence and uncertainty.
- Wing architectures: literature comparison of fixed, delta, swept and variable-sweep concepts, including endurance and mass tradeoffs.
- Structural research: verify introductory finite-element benchmarks and document material sources.
- Propulsion: literature comparison of broad technologies, without declaring a selected configuration.
- Subsonic flight dynamics: simulation, sensor errors, disturbances and basic instrument validation.
- Regulatory and ethical oversight: legal authority, personnel safety and public release reviews.

## Minimum standard for each technical report
Research question; author/reviewer/date; references; versioned input data; model and software versions; assumptions; verification tests; limitations; uncertainties; decision or next step.

## Initial deliverables
AEQ-MAT-001 standard-atmosphere validation; AEQ-CFD-001 published flow benchmark; AEQ-TRADE-001 wing concept review; AEQ-PROP-001 technology literature assessment.

No simulation result is evidence of actual aircraft performance unless independently validated.
