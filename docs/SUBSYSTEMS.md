**Important:** This is a research work-breakdown structure. No propulsion, wing design, structural architecture, vehicle mass or supersonic capability is selected or verified.

## Review questions for each subsystem
- What is known, and what is assumed?
- What independent evidence supports the model?
- Which uncertainty prevents a decision?
- What data could falsify the hypothesis?
- What is safe and appropriate to publish?
