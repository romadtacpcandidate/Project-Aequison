# AEQ-RES-003 — Academic Research, Competition and Commercial Separation
**Revision 0.1 · Draft · 2026-10-08**

AEQUISON is an independent team research program with three possible pathways:
- **Graduate research:** hypothesis-driven work, faculty supervision if admitted, ethical research and publications.
- **Boom Prize:** independent competition compliance, eligibility, measurement and relevant aviation permissions.
- **Potential commercial applications:** separately negotiated intellectual property and lawful future business activities.

These pathways are related but must **not** be presumed to have shared ownership, unrestricted funding, or compatible licensing.

## Required reviews before institutional or commercial use
- University thesis acceptance and adviser scope
- University IP, facilities, publication and sponsored-research policies
- Team invention ownership, authorship, confidentiality and contribution agreements
- Boom eligibility, including institutional and corporate-resource restrictions
- Software-license permitted use, including partner-held ANSYS Mechanical
- Publication/export-control and confidential-data classification, with qualified counsel if applicable

## Publication policy
Academic manuscripts can be published only with author permission, validated methods, appropriate data rights and required institutional review. Proprietary material may require a separate private documentation track.

Nothing in this page transfers IP, creates a partnership, or offers legal advice.
