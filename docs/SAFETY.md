# AEQ-SAF-001 — Safety, Scope & Authorization
**Rev 0.1 | Draft**

The initial AEQUISON research phase covers literature studies, numerical simulations, verification, non-flight research experiments, and separately approved low-speed demonstrators.

Competition registration alone is not flight authorization. No authority to conduct supersonic operations has been established.

Before any physical flight, obtain a written safety review and applicable airspace/aviation approvals with qualified personnel. Maintain a risk register, reporting process, and stop-work procedure.

Risk categories include third-party injury, airspace, system failure, propulsion hazards, model error, cybersecurity, funding/eligibility, data confidentiality, and regulatory changes.

Public material must not represent unvalidated simulations as measured flight outcomes.
