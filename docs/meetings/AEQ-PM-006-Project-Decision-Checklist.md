> **Purpose:** Use these **17 decision topics** as a shared reference to establish AEQUISON's research direction, preliminary requirements, feasibility review, roles, resources, and first action items. This is a decision checklist, **not** a read-aloud script. The team can cover it over multiple meetings.

## How to record every topic

Mark each topic **Decided**, **Needs research**, or **Deferred**. For any decision, log the actual agreement, owner, supporting rationale/evidence, due date, and reviewer. **Discussing an item does not constitute approval.** Names, financial commitments, contact details, unreleased designs, and sensitive notes belong in a controlled private workspace.

## Priority outcomes for the first meeting

If time is limited, prioritize these five decisions before anything else:

1. **Mission:** Is AEQUISON fundamentally research, competition, or both?
2. **Preliminary requirements:** What will the project investigate, without prematurely approving an aircraft design?
3. **Technical feasibility:** Which unknowns must be researched before committing major funds?
4. **Responsibilities:** Confirm design/CAD and CAD/FEA responsibilities, plus assignments for uncovered workstreams.
5. **First 30 days:** Approve a small set of specific research deliverables, each with owner, review method, and due date.
